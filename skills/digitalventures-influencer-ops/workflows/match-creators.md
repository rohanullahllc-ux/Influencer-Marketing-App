# Match Creators

## Objective
Recommend qualified creators for a specific campaign objective.

## Procedure
1. Exclude creators who are not qualified.
2. Compare audience fit.
3. Compare content fit.
4. Compare product fit.
5. Compare commercial fit.
6. Identify evidence supporting each recommendation.
7. Identify important uncertainties.
8. Provide a ranked recommendation only when the user explicitly requests ranking; otherwise use grouped recommendations.

## Output
For each recommended creator provide creator, fit explanation, evidence, uncertainty, recommended campaign angle, and suggested next action.
