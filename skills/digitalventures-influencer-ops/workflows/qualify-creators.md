# Qualify Creators

## Objective
Determine whether discovered creators satisfy explicit campaign qualification criteria.

## Procedure
1. Load product and campaign requirements.
2. Load creator evidence.
3. Evaluate audience fit.
4. Evaluate content fit.
5. Evaluate product fit.
6. Evaluate commercial fit.
7. Evaluate obvious quality or risk concerns.
8. Record evidence and reasons.
9. Set one status: qualified, not_qualified, or needs_review.

## Rule
Do not reduce qualification to follower count.
Every decision must be explainable from observed evidence.
Creators marked needs_review must not be treated as approved matches.
