# Campaign Report

## Objective
Turn stored campaign activity and conversion data into a decision-ready report.

## Report sections
1. Campaign objective
2. Creator outreach volume
3. Reply rate
4. Qualified and active creators
5. Content/activity where available
6. Clicks where available
7. Orders where available
8. Revenue where available
9. Conversion metrics where calculable
10. Strongest observed patterns
11. Weakest observed patterns
12. Data gaps
13. Recommended next actions

## Metric rules
Use observed data for raw metrics.
Calculated metrics must show their inputs or formula when useful.
Do not estimate missing business results unless the user explicitly requests a scenario model; clearly label scenarios as estimates.
