# Process Creator Reply

## Objective
Convert an inbound creator response into structured intelligence and a safe next action.

## Classification
- interested
- question
- negotiation
- needs_information
- declined
- not_now
- wrong_contact
- unclear

## Procedure
1. Preserve the original reply.
2. Classify it.
3. Extract actionable facts.
4. Compare against campaign and offer state.
5. Draft a response when appropriate.
6. Mark external responses pending approval.
7. Update CRM state without inventing information.
