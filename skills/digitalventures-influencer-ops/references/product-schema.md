- price

## Product Intelligence

- value_proposition

- creator_profile
- campaign_angles
- messaging_angles

- claims_to_avoid
- source_material
- confidence
- status
- generated_at
- approved_at

## Rules
Product facts describe what is known about the product.
Product Intelligence contains analysis derived from those facts.
Do not convert assumptions into product facts.
Do not create unsupported claims, guarantees, statistics, testimonials, performance promises, or customer outcomes.
Product Intelligence must be marked approved before it becomes campaign-ready.
