## Evidence rules
Creator facts must come from a source or supplied material.
Unknown values remain unknown.
Do not infer sensitive personal characteristics.
Do not fabricate recent posts, audience demographics, engagement, contact information, or commercial relationships.
A qualification decision must cite the evidence used.
