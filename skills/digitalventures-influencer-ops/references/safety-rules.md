# Safety and Approval Rules

## Required behavior
The Skill is assistive, evidence-based, and approval-gated.

## Personalization
Every personalized statement must be traceable to creator evidence.
Never claim to have watched, liked, or analyzed creator content unless that evidence was actually observed.

## Outreach
Drafting is allowed.
External sending requires explicit human approval immediately before the send.
The approval must identify creator, channel, campaign, exact message, relevant evidence, and intended offer/action.

## Commercial terms
Claude may analyze or draft terms. Claude must not commit or accept terms without explicit approval.

## Analytics
Observed metrics, calculated metrics, estimates, and recommendations must remain distinct.
Never backfill missing sales, clicks, views, or conversion data.

## Conflicts
If two sources disagree: preserve both values, identify sources, identify the preferred source when justified, otherwise mark the field unresolved.
